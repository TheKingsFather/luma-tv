Regression reproduction
- The new channelPlaybackHidesControlsWithoutRemoteInput device test failed
  against 0.6.0 with: Playback controls did not hide without moving focus.
- Real local video was playing, the Pause button had focus, and no remote input
  was sent before checking the fade timeout.
- Reproduction output: controls-reproduction-0.6.0.log.

Fix
- Arm the six-second timer when actual playback starts or resumes.
- Cancel the previous timer when changing sources.
- Only schedule fading for visible full-player controls while playing and with
  no player dialog open; pausing cancels the timer.
- Ignore playing-state callbacks from replaced or released players.
